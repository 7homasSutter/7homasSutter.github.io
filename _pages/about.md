I am a PhD Student in Computer Science at the <a href='https://www.unibe.ch' target="_blank">University of Bern</a>,
supervised by <a href='https://seg.inf.unibe.ch/team/timo-kehrer/' target="_blank">Prof. Dr. Timo Kehrer</a>.
My doctoral research focuses on **automated software testing** and **dynamic security analysis of mobile applications** —
developing scalable methods to analyse Android systems at the firmware, framework, and application layer.

I am also a Research Associate in the
<a href="https://www.zhaw.ch/en/engineering/institutes-centres/init/information-security/" target="_blank">Information Security Group (ISE)</a>
at the <a href="https://www.zhaw.ch" target="_blank">Zurich University of Applied Sciences (ZHAW)</a>,
where I work on applied security research at the intersection of academia and industry.

**Research interests:** mobile security · Android firmware analysis · automated testing · phishing awareness · software engineering
